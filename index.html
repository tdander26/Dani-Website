Our vision

Your number one asset is your health.

Our goal is simple: make this community the healthiest it can be. When we feel good, we perform better and do the things we want to do — from picking up grandchildren, to gardening, to pitching a full game without pain.

At Robertson Chiropractic we give you and your family the tools to take the first step toward improved function, better performance and overall wellness. When we feel good, we do good — and a community of happy people can accomplish amazing things.

Our services

Care that meets your body where it is.

Seven complementary tools, combined in the way your body responds to best — often within the same visit.

01

Chiropractic Adjustments

A safe, gentle technique using the hands or a hand-held instrument to re-introduce normal movement back into the spine and joints.

02

Acupuncture

A safe, effective way to treat inflamed, irritated and injured muscles and tissue. This ancient medicine is modified to you — including needle-free options.

03

Trigger Point Therapy

Focused release for the knots that keep coming back — used alone or alongside adjustments and acupuncture.

04

Soft Tissue & Muscular Release

Hands-on work through muscle, fascia and scar tissue to restore glide, length and blood flow where movement has been guarded.

05

SOT — Sacro-Occipital Technique

A very gentle corrective method that identifies patterns of whole-body imbalance and corrects them using blocks, wedges or adjustments.

06

Webster Technique

A specific analysis and adjustment for pregnancy — balancing the pelvis and surrounding ligaments so mom is more comfortable and baby has room.

07

Athletic Functional Screening

An effective way to identify mechanical problems — sometimes before injury occurs. Squats, lunges and sport-specific work, supervised, to find the weak link.

What to expect

Your first visit, start to finish.

No mystery, no pressure, and no surprise conversations about money.

  1. 01

    Fill out your forms

    Download and complete the new patient paperwork and bring it with you. If that isn't possible, forms are waiting at the office — arriving a few minutes early does the same job.

  2. 02

    Book a time that actually works

    Call or message the office and we'll find a slot that fits around work, school pickup or practice.

  3. 03

    A thorough one-on-one evaluation

    Dr. Robertson performs a complete musculoskeletal evaluation and asks about diet, exercise and sleep. Then you talk goals, because the plan is built backwards from them.

  4. 04

    Your file gets reviewed

    Dr. Robertson reviews everything and determines whether you're a good candidate for chiropractic care — and tells you plainly if you're not.

  5. 05

    Results, plan and financials

    At your follow-up you receive your new patient packet, walk through the exam findings, discuss financials openly and ask every question you've been saving up.
